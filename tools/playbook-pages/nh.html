Nighthaven is a weak map for us (44.6% squad aggregate, 0-2 and 3-8 rounds on Oct 23–26), so we play one fixed plan per site. Core idea: battle for IT. Keep the outer IT walls shut, buy time in the top-floor rooms for about 1:30, then fall back into site and play for trades.

Attack: B Tank / Animus

Attackpro 46.8% — best attack site

Clear Tank from above through the soft Control floor, flame the two doors, drop the hatch and tuck-plant in the corner while Roman denies Cargo stairs.

Step by step

  1. Prep: drone on an IT shelf; drone Storage + Cargo; shoot default cams.
  2. Roman scans outside rooms so Sanya enters safe.
  3. Sanya in through the Rafters window; Airjab 1 high on the far IT door.
  4. Down Warehouse Stairs: Airjab 2 far Storage door, Airjab 3 above Cargo door.
  5. Dima / Renat hard-breach 1F Warehouse hatch + top of Cargo stairs hatch.
  6. Nikita cuts long lines in the Control floor: window line, along the wall, middle.
  7. Roman works down Cargo stairs; no over-extending.
  8. Drone the drop zone — defenders hide behind the tank and swing late.
  9. Renat flames both door frames; Sanya drops and plants in the corner.
  10. Hold: Renat + Nikita on holes, Roman on Cargo stairs, Dima on the hatch / flank.

    Pro notes · W7M vs Space Station

    from video

    How W7M went 7-1 on Nighthaven against the best NA team. Garage = Warehouse (top garage = Rafters), Aqua = Blue/CC Stairs, Ebox = Electrical, Garden = Connector side. Source: coach breakdown.

    Defence, general

    • Ban two hard breachers (Ace / Thermite / Hibana): the map is decided by breaches (IT, garden, pillar, garage)
    • Kaid / Bandit banned → Mira on the IT wall: a Thermite on it breaks when the Mira pops; C4 / impacts can be lined up through it
    • Read the attack type ("triggers") and move bodies: garage vs garden take on 2F, site hit vs roam clear in basement

    2F CC / Servers · 5-man top extension

    • Smoke holds Aqua (power spot); Castle on the bottom Aqua door
    • Azami in top garage, Clash around Ebox / pillar (counters the Blackbeard hop), Mozzie notifications
    • Ebox = key anchor (close shotgun / Mute); Ebox + pillar = crossfire on the garden entry
    • Two takes: garage (IT + top garage → Aqua sandwich) or garden / split (Meeting → garden breach → Ebox, Buck underneath, IT late)
    • Vs garden take: the garage player drops the hatch and flanks the Buck on 1F
    • Garage hold: Castles bottom garage, lower garage walls double reinforced, 2 players in garage, rotate flipped for a long angle into site
    • Adjustment: garage double reinforced + 1 flex reinforcement so the player can give it up; Mira instead of Clash / Castle

    Basement · 2-2-1 roam

    • 2 on 2F (Azami top garage + Lesion), 2 on 1F (Warden on Storage holes, one in Control), site player in Exo, not site
    • Animus wall left soft, both doors castled = killbox; hatch above opened to threaten
    • Castle the 90 window: splits the map into kitchen half / garage half
    • Garage walls double reinforced; Warden on the vert so flashes / smokes can't stop him
    • Adjustment: lighter roam on the Aqua side (Mute top floor, Recruit C4 + barbed), garage single reinforced, Castle on the doorway that cuts the Aqua rotate

    On the board: Basement → Option 3.

    1F Kitchen · scrappy, 5 reinforcements

    • Warden (later Smoke) on Lobby Stairs holds top + bottom Lobby; one player at Harry Potter
    • Frost with shield on 90; Fenrir covers Vending / the back; Azami on site
    • Castles on the Storage door side; reinforce the Freezer hatch from outside early; feet holes along the Freezer wall
    • One aggressive one-for-one player at the garden breach

    Attack ideas

    • Soft pressure: open IT, garden and bottom garage early so defenders lose mobility and can't read the hit
    • Basement roam clear: Ace through the garage door, Blackbeard hops the Ebox window, Ram doubles up, hard breacher fast through Aqua, one player in bottom garage cuts fall-backs
    • Storage bait and switch: fake top-floor clear, Sledge + Blackbeard up Cargo Stairs, lurk from Lobby to 90, Ace holds the cut from a window, Hibana shotgun takes Aqua, plant in the tuck spot (on the board: Storage → Attack B)
    • Keep one lurk who takes free space slowly

    Operator bans

    We ban only the opponent's side. Bans stay until sides switch, then reset. Use the slots in order: one per round in ranked, or two + one in the pro format.

    BanWe attackWe defend CCWe defend B / Storage
    1KaidAceAce
    2BanditThermite / HibanaThermite / Hibana
    3Azami (B) / Mira (CC, Storage)GrimRam

    Why

    • Kaid, Bandit: hold hatches and walls our B execute needs open; pros' top opening bans here (33 / 25 of 40 maps).
    • Azami: barriers plug Nikita's holes and Sanya's hatch.
    • Ace + Thermite / Hibana: double hard-breach ban, like W7M. Nighthaven is decided by breaches (IT, garden, pillar, garage); with two hard breachers gone the attack can't open them all.
    • Ram: 54% of pro B attack rounds, opens Storage/Control from above.
    • Grim: most banned attacker (158), flushes Rafters and Mezz.
    • Montagne: +4.6 win delta; ban him instead of Grim if shields keep taking Water/Rafters.

    Adapt ban 3

    • Plant denied by fire → Goyo
    • Floor holes blocked → Azami
    • Roamer scanned out → Solid Snake

    Our backups if they ban us

    • Sledge ↔ Buck → Ram · Grim → Ying · Azami → Ela · Kaid → Bandit
